# 📚 **Phase 4: Capstone Application (Advanced E-Commerce & Leaderboards) & Consulting Acumen**

**Outcome:** Build a complex, scalable e-commerce platform for your game and a real-time leaderboard, showcasing your full technical mastery. Learn to leverage your expertise for high-value client solutions as a freelancer.

### 1. **Capstone Application Project: Scalable E-Commerce Platform & Leaderboards for "Arcane Runes"**

- [ ] [**Fullstack Open Specialization**](https://fullstackopen.com/en/ "null"): Complete this specialization to build a modern full-stack application and consolidate your knowledge, applying it directly to your game's e-commerce.
    
- [ ] **E-Commerce Platform Development:**
    
    - [ ] **Action:** Build the core backend services (API) for products, carts, orders, and user accounts for your game's e-commerce store.
        
    - [ ] **Action:** Develop the full-stack platform, focusing on scalability and reliability, deploying it as interconnected microservices on your Kubernetes cluster.
        
    - [ ] **Action:** Implement a **Blue-Green Deployment** strategy for zero-downtime updates to your e-commerce store.
        
    - [ ] **Action:** Seamlessly integrate the e-commerce functionality directly into your "Arcane Runes" game's UI and website. **Ensure integration with the Player Profile to display purchase history and owned items.**
        
- [ ] **Real-time Leaderboard System:**
    
    - [ ] **Action:** Develop a **real-time leaderboard system** to track player scores and achievements within "Arcane Runes."
        
    - [ ] **Integration:** Integrate this system with your game's data and **display it prominently on your Player Profile web dashboard and the game's website.**
        
- [ ] **Microtransactions :**
    
    - [ ] **Action:** Refine your blockchain integration for advanced microtransaction models, potentially including the sale and transfer of **NFTs for unique in-game assets** through your e-commerce store, with these assets visible on the **Player Profile**.
        
- **Action:** This project serves as your ultimate capstone, applying all learned skills (full-stack, DevOps, infrastructure management, blockchain specialization) to build a robust, large-scale system that runs entirely on your Unified IT Environment.
